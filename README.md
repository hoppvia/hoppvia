## Overview

Hoppvia helps local transport businesses launch a branded booking and dispatch service without building custom software from scratch. Operators can offer a customer app, a driver app, and a dispatch platform under their own brand, while keeping the operational flow in one system.

This is a fit for businesses that want:

- white-label taxi booking apps
- private hire booking software
- limo and black car dispatch software
- chauffeur booking software
- ride-hailing software for local operators
- taxi fleet management software

The platform is designed for operators who want a branded customer experience, lower operational overhead, and a simpler pricing model than per-trip or per-vehicle alternatives.

## What's included

### Customer app (iOS and Android)
- Published under your brand name and logo
- Pickup and destination search
- Ride types and fare preview before booking
- Live GPS tracking of the assigned driver
- In-app card payments through your own payment gateway account
- Trip status, receipts, ratings, and ride history

### Driver app (iOS and Android)
- Go online or offline
- Accept or decline ride offers
- View pickup, destination, and expected earnings
- Arrival, pickup code check, and trip completion
- Trip history and earnings

### Admin and dispatch panel (web)
- Bookings, free drivers, and active rides in one view
- Live map of driver locations
- Offer a ride to a chosen driver
- Restart the driver search or cancel before pickup with a reason
- Ride types, fares, trip records, and driver earnings

### Maps
Choose Hoppvia's built-in maps to avoid Google Maps API bills, or connect your own Google Maps account and pay Google directly.

## FAQs

### How much does a white-label taxi app cost with Hoppvia?
Starter is $1,990 a year for up to 50 drivers, and Growth is $3,990 a year for 51–200 drivers. Both plans include a $499 one-time setup fee.

### Does Hoppvia take a commission on rides?
No. Every plan includes 0% commission and unlimited trips.

### How long does it take to launch?
About a week. Once your logo, colors, and developer accounts are ready, your apps can go to the App Store and Google Play.

### Do I need to pay for Google Maps?
No. You can use Hoppvia's built-in maps. If you prefer Google Maps, you can connect your own account and pay Google directly.

### Is the source code included?
No. Hoppvia is a hosted, licensed product. Hosting and updates are included in every plan.

### Which countries do you serve?
Hoppvia serves taxi, private hire, limousine, and chauffeur operators in the UK, US, UAE, and other markets.
