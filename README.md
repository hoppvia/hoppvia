## What's included

### Customer app (iOS and Android)
- Published under your brand name and logo
- Pickup and destination search, ride types and fare before booking
- Live GPS tracking of the assigned driver
- In-app card payments through your own payment gateway account
- Trip status, receipts, ratings and ride history

### Driver app (iOS and Android)
- Go online or offline
- Ride offers showing pickup, destination and expected earnings, with accept and decline
- Arrival, pickup code check and trip completion
- Trip history and earnings

### Admin and dispatch panel (web)
- Bookings, free drivers and active rides in one view
- Live map of driver locations
- Offer a ride to a chosen driver; the trip is assigned once the driver accepts
- Restart the driver search or cancel before pickup, with a note of why
- Ride types, fares, trip records and driver earnings

### Maps
Choose Hoppvia's **built-in maps**, which avoid Google Maps API bills, or connect **your own Google Maps account** and pay Google directly.

## FAQ

**How much does a white-label taxi app cost with Hoppvia?**
Starter is $1,990 a year for up to 50 drivers, and Growth is $3,990 a year for 51–200 drivers. Both have a $499 one-time setup fee.

**Does Hoppvia take a commission on rides?**
No. Every plan has 0% commission and unlimited trips.

**How long does it take to launch?**
About a week. Once your logo, colours and developer accounts are ready, your apps go to the App Store and Google Play.

**Do I need to pay for Google Maps?**
No. You can use Hoppvia's built-in maps. If you prefer Google Maps, you connect your own account and pay Google directly.

**Is the source code included?**
No. Hoppvia is a hosted, licensed product. Hosting and updates are included in every plan.

**Which countries do you serve?**
Taxi, private hire, limousine and chauffeur operators in the UK, US and UAE, and other markets.
